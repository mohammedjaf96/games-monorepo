Privacy Policy

Effective date:

1. No account, no login

Blocko, Dashy, and Mergo (the "Games") do not require creating an account and do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information. There is no server-side player profile.

2. What is stored, and where

Your progress (best score, coins earned, gems, owned/selected cosmetics, settings, daily-reward streak) is stored locally on your device only. It is never uploaded to us. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this progress is permanently lost — we cannot recover it.

3. Advertising

The Games are funded entirely by in-app advertising served through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partner networks may collect and process:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID, Apple's IDFA where permitted)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, completions)

This data is governed by Google's own privacy policy and by each mediated ad network's policy. We do not receive or store this data ourselves.

Consent (EEA / UK / Switzerland)

On first launch, a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form may be shown where required by law, letting you choose whether ads are personalized.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, you will be asked for permission before the app (via AdMob) accesses the identifier used for cross-app tracking. You can allow or deny this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Children's privacy

The Games are not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The only data shared with third parties is the advertising data described in §3, shared with Google AdMob and its ad network partners for the sole purpose of serving and measuring ads.

6. Your choices

  • You can reset ad personalization or opt out of interest-based ads through your device's ad settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising / Tracking).
  • You can uninstall the app at any time, which removes all locally stored data.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the Games after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the developer contact link on this app's store listing (Google Play / App Store).
