# Privacy Policy

**Apps covered:** Blocko, Dashy, Mergo (the "Games")
**Effective date:** [FILL IN BEFORE PUBLISHING]

This policy explains what data the Games collect, why, and what choices you
have. Host this file at a public URL (e.g. GitHub Pages, or your own domain)
and use that URL in each app's store listing — Google Play and the App Store
both require a reachable privacy-policy link for any app that shows ads.

## 1. No account, no login
The Games do not require creating an account and do not collect your name,
email address, or any other personally identifying information. There is no
server-side player profile.

## 2. What is stored, and where
Your progress (best score, coins earned, gems, owned/selected cosmetics,
settings, daily-reward streak) is stored **locally on your device only**,
using local storage (Hive). It is never uploaded to us. If you uninstall the
app or clear its data, this progress is permanently lost — we cannot recover
it.

## 3. Advertising
The Games are funded entirely by in-app advertising served through **Google
AdMob**. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partner networks may collect
and process:
- Device and advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID, Apple's
  IDFA where permitted)
- Approximate location (derived from IP address)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, completions)

This data is governed by Google's own privacy policy
(https://policies.google.com/privacy) and by each mediated ad network's
policy. We do not receive or store this data ourselves.

### Consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland)
On first launch, a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form may be
shown where required by law, letting you choose whether ads are personalized.

### App Tracking Transparency (iOS)
On iOS, you will be asked for permission before the app (via AdMob) accesses
the identifier used for cross-app tracking. You can allow or deny this at
any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

## 4. Children's privacy
The Games are not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the
equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children. If the app's audience or store listing
targets children, ad serving must instead be configured for
Google-certified child-directed treatment (tags_for_child_directed_treatment)
and personalized ads must be disabled for that audience — update this
section and the AdMob configuration accordingly before publishing to a
children's category.

## 5. Data sharing
We do not sell your data. The only data shared with third parties is the
advertising data described in §3, shared with Google AdMob and its ad
network partners for the sole purpose of serving and measuring ads.

## 6. Your choices
- You can reset ad personalization or opt out of interest-based ads through
  your device's ad settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS:
  Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising / Tracking).
- You can uninstall the app at any time, which removes all locally stored
  data.

## 7. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the Games
after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
